Readme skin lesions
30.03.23
These codelists was designed as a negative control for analyses in CPRD Gold and Aurum. It is supposed to be a list of skin lesions that are not related to taking antiplatelets, that people might present to their GP with. It was compliled by J Lee, a GP. 

It is designed to have a wide range of skin lesions. This encompases cancerous and non cancerous, acne, abscesses, skin tags (external), dermatophytosis (but not candidiasis), bullae, warts, aktinic keratoses, and some treatments and investigations (excision, biopsy, cryotherapy). It doesn't (or shouldn't) contain skin grafts, congenital skin problems, injuries, descriptions of turgor and other non lesion type rashes. Fotr example I have excluded results of biopsies related to antibodies, red bits of skin around stomas, ulcers, history of past lesions, contact dermatitis, and other things less localised. Burns and foreign bodies are excluded. Skin sensations too. Paediatric stuff is not relevant to my adult population.
